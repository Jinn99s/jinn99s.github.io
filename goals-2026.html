        Run 4.2 miles under 50:58

        Baseline 62:00 → Aug 27 test: 56:23 → Target 50:58  |  Due Dec 31, 2026
        Your Aug 27 test changes the picture: 4.2mi in 56:23 already beats the original Sep 8 checkpoint (under 60:00) by a wide margin and is closing in on the Oct 20 target (under 56:00) — in week 3. That's a fast start.

        But the effort behind it matters as much as the time. Heart rate sitting above 170 from ~26 minutes and climbing to 180–188 for the last stretch is a near-maximal effort for most people — that's race-day intensity, not an "easy base run." Doing that weekly instead of building an aerobic base is how people burn out or get hurt by week 6–8, even if the watch says they're getting faster right now.

        So the plan below shifts: most weekly runs should feel conversational — you could hold a conversation without gasping. Save hard, near-max efforts for the specific tempo/interval/time-trial days already built into the Build and Sharpen phases. And since you're already ahead of the original targets, the checkpoints below are pulled in to build in more cushion before Dec 31, not to push you harder sooner.
        SMART: Specific — run 4.2 miles in under 50:58, with a stretch target of under 50:00 for cushion. Measurable — time trial logged below each test. Achievable — you're already 4 minutes ahead of the original Sep 8 pace target, so the remaining gap to 50:58 is smaller than it looked a week ago. Relevant — benchmarked against your Aug 27 test. Time-bound — Dec 31, 2026.
